# Round country flags

Source: https://github.com/HatScripts/circle-flags

Additional flags for every country in `src/lib/world-countries.ts` were imported
from commit `379588b5da95482d6bbf10bd45644a35b0609ea6`.
The original 26 local assets are preserved. See LICENSE.md for the MIT license.

These files are served locally, so country pickers and trip cards do not depend
on an external image service or the platform's emoji font.
